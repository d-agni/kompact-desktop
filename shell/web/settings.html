Assistant

Siva does what you ask: click its ring in the menu bar and speak, or type. It runs on this PC; nothing it hears or reads leaves this computer.

In the menu barThe ring turns into a sound wave while it listens. Control Center has the same choice.

Voice

What it may do

Volume, music, windows, desktops, Do Not Disturb, dark mode, opening appsDone at once, with Undo.
Closing windows, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, logging out, sleep, restart, power offIt asks first, every time, and only a click on its button says yes; saying "yes" doesn't.
Anything elseIt can't: no files, no passwords, no installing, no internet.

Routines

Several requests under one name. Say “set up for coding”, “start focus” or “movie time”; the plan shows as it runs, and one Undo takes it all back.
